# First 100k Sprint Plan — 30 Days

By Essy Udeme — Why Not Build?  ·  whynotbuild

**Days 1-3** List three things you have already made. Pick the one with visible proof.

**Days 4-7** Post the proof. Add one line: "I can do this for you." No deck, no tiers.

**Days 8-14** Reply to every reply. Quote confidently. Deliver fast.

**Days 15-21** Ask each client for one referral and one testimonial sentence.

**Days 22-30** Repackage what sold twice into a fixed-price offer.

Record after: what converted, what wasted a week, what you'd charge now.
