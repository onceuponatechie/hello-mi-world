# Essay Skeleton — Notice, Question, Test, Tell

By Essy Udeme — Why Not Build?  ·  whynotbuild

**Notice** — the specific thing you saw. Concrete, dated, yours.

**Question** — what it made you ask. One sentence, no hedging.

**Test** — what you did to find out. Small and real.

**Tell** — what changed, including what you got wrong.

**Takeaway** — one action the reader can take this week.
