# Product Teardown Template — 90 Minutes

By Essy Udeme — Why Not Build?  ·  whynotbuild

**0-10 min** Write the question you're answering. One sentence.

**10-35 min** Use it as a first-time user. Screenshot every screen. Time the gap to first real action.

**35-60 min** Note the three best decisions and the three worst. Say why, not that you like them.

**60-75 min** Find the trade-off. Every good decision costs something — name the cost.

**75-90 min** Write the verdict in two sentences and the one move you'd steal.
