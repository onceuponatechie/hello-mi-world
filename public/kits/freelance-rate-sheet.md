# Freelance Rate & Scope Sheet

By Essy Udeme — Why Not Build?  ·  whynotbuild

**Deliverable:** exactly what the client receives, in nouns.

**Not included:** three items, named.

**Revisions:** two rounds. Further rounds billed at ____ per round.

**Timeline:** starts on deposit, not on conversation.

**Payment:** 50% to begin, 50% on delivery.

**Rate logic:** price the outcome you've already produced once, not the hours you'll spend learning.
