# Pitch Deck Outline — 12 Slides

By Essy Udeme — Why Not Build?  ·  whynotbuild

One claim per slide. Evidence directly beneath it. Readable in 3 seconds with the presenter silent.

1. Title — company, one-line what.
2. The problem — specific, human, dated.
3. Why now — what changed in the world.
4. The wedge — the narrow first thing.
5. Product — one screen, one flow.
6. Evidence — the strongest number you have.
7. Market — bottom-up, not top-down.
8. Business model — how money moves.
9. Competition — honest positioning axes.
10. Team — why you specifically.
11. Traction & plan — next 12 months.
12. The ask — amount, use, milestones.
