# Portfolio Audit Checklist

By Essy Udeme — Why Not Build?  ·  whynotbuild

### First three seconds
- [ ] What you do is legible without scrolling
- [ ] Who it's for is named
- [ ] One clear next action

### Each project
- [ ] The problem stated before the solution
- [ ] Your specific role, not the team's
- [ ] At least one number or before/after
- [ ] A decision you made and its trade-off
- [ ] What you got wrong
- [ ] Real images, not mockup templates

### Craft
- [ ] Loads under 3s on mobile data
- [ ] Works with one thumb
- [ ] No lorem ipsum, anywhere
- [ ] Contact route that actually works
- [ ] Every link resolves
- [ ] Copy free of "passionate", "seamless", "innovative"

### Proof
- [ ] Something published, not just described
- [ ] Dates present and recent
- [ ] A way to verify at least one claim
