# Product Brief — One Pager

By Essy Udeme — Why Not Build?  ·  whynotbuild

## 1. The problem
Who has it, how often, and what they do today instead.

## 2. The person
One sentence. If you can't name where to reach them, stop here.

## 3. The bet
"We believe that if we ____, then ____ will happen, because ____."

## 4. Smallest shippable version
What can exist in two weeks and still be judged fairly?

## 5. How we'll know
One number, one qualitative signal, one date.

## 6. Explicitly not doing
List three things. Protect them.
