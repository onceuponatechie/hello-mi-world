# Project conventions

- This is the Next.js App Router replica of `onceuponatechie/butter-canvas-dream`, source commit `eb90cfa`.
- Keep homepage sections available through hash navigation. The Builds collection lives at `/builds`, with individual studies at `/builds/[slug]`; links from inner pages must reach homepage sections correctly.
- Current Builds are approved concept examples, not verified client work. Keep assumptions, proposed outputs and testing status explicit; do not invent research findings, launches or performance results.
- Tools & Templates lives at `/tools-and-templates`. Show 12 products, with six downloadable products and six labelled Planned. Only ready products have detail routes under `/tools-and-templates/[slug]`; their files live in `public/downloads`. Keep direct downloads available without an email gate. Collection counts are derived from product data.
- Tools & Templates copy sounds like an experienced, curious learner, not an expert or beginner. Include the reader in the questions and use first person sparingly; avoid repetitive “I” statements and third-person author descriptions such as “this book by Essy Udeme”. Keep file instructions clear and ground personal statements in supplied writing.
- Contact lives at `/contact`; Say hi and footer CTAs lead there. The form prepares a local email draft, not a sent-message confirmation. Keep direct mail links available through `site.email`.
- Stories / Why Not Build lives at `/stories` with seven articles at `/stories/[slug]` across six publication categories. Keep both supplied posts verbatim: the Product Manager post dated 11 August 2026 and “8 Skills I'm Betting My Career On” dated 14 October 2026. Calculate read times from the body; their full lengths are exceptions to the 2–4-minute target for the five scenario-based pieces. The skills post uses the supplied JPEG cover and the owner's original What Should I Get Good At? EPUB with its supplied cover. Each article must reference a ready Tools & Templates product with an actual direct download. Resource cards stay black with green buttons. Products can be shared across stories; keep the twelve-product collection and six downloads. Do not fabricate first-person experiences in new articles.
- Research Vault lives at `/research-vault`, with four studies at `/research-vault/[slug]`. Cite primary sources next to documented claims, distinguish interpretations and examples, and retain methods and limits. Do not invent interviews, tests or findings. The Nigeria chart uses a dated World Bank/ITU snapshot in `public/research`; retain its source precision, attribution and reproducible calculation script.
- Preserve source typography, spacing, images, canvas composition, and animation timing.
- The owner replaced PR #20's personal bento with a featured ebook after Services and before the newsletter. Keep a black two-column surround with the original ebook cover on the left and a slightly tilted charcoal information panel on the right; stack these on mobile. Download the owner's exact existing EPUB without an email gate. Do not restore the sample reading grid, music embed, product practice cards or adventure carousel.
- About lives at `/about` and replaces Services in desktop, mobile and footer navigation. The homepage Services section stays at `#services`. Use the supplied portrait and biographical facts from the owner's writing (marketing, design, product management, research and writing); do not invent employers, qualifications, clients or personal history.
- Keep the Why Not Build feature as a 360×486 Canvas 2D composition with DOM controls and a semantic fallback.
- Never rewrite published Git history.
- Before pushing changes, run lint, typecheck, and the production build.
